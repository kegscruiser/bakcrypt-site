BakCrypt privacy policy

BakCrypt is a personal Android app for the private use of its developer.

What the app accesses

With the user's consent, the app accesses the user's own Google Drive (the drive scope) to read and write the user's own Cryptomator vault, and it reads files on the device that the user chooses to back up.

What is collected and shared

The developer does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any user data. Files are encrypted on the device before upload and are sent only to the user's own Google Drive. The app has no analytics, advertising or third-party servers. The vault password and access tokens stay on the device.

Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
